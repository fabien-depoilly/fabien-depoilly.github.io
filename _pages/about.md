I am a PhD graduate in fluid mechanics, currently seeking a postdoctoral position. I defended my PhD thesis on June 30, 2026 at the [Laboratoire de Mécanique des Fluides et d'Acoustique](https://lmfa.ec-lyon.fr/) (LMFA, UMR5509, Université Lyon 1), and was previously a Temporary Teaching and Research Assistant (ATER) at [Polytech Lyon](https://polytech.univ-lyon1.fr/) until August 2026.

My research focuses on the **stability of thin-film flows of non-Newtonian fluids down an inclined plane** — a problem relevant to numerous natural and industrial contexts, from avalanches and mudflows to coating processes. Given the diversity of rheological behaviours encountered in practice, my work aims to build a **unifying theoretical framework**, independent of any specific rheological law, and to confront it with experimental data obtained on an inclined channel.

My PhD thesis, *Stability of Non-Newtonian Fluid Film Flows on an Inclined Plane*, was supervised by Hamda Ben Hadid, with Séverine Millet and Simon Dagois-Bohy as co-supervisors.
